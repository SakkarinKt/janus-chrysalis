## Learned

- **Confound fix confirmed** (`self_checked, high confidence`): every non-baseline radius's
  pre-freeze records are now bit-identical to the `viewRadius=2` baseline's, same seed and
  condition (9/9 cross-radius checks `identical: true`, alongside the usual 9/9
  control-vs-intervention checks). Pre-freeze training is provably unaffected by which post-freeze
  radius a run will use — the exact gap 2026-08-22's whole-episode sweep couldn't close.
- **The non-monotonicity survives the fix** (`self_checked, high confidence` on the numbers,
  `medium confidence` on the interpretation): mean `|diffMean|` still goes 0.0324 → 0.1799 → 0.1514
  across radii 2/4/6, with the pre-freeze-training channel now closed off by construction. This
  rules out 2026-08-22's leading hypothesis as the *whole* explanation for the whole-episode
  sweep's non-monotonicity, though it doesn't rule it out as a partial contributor there. Seed
  1003's `diffMean` swings even harder here (`0.0000` → `+0.4169` → `-0.4351`) than in the
  confounded sweep, which if anything favors "this is `n=3` sampling noise" over "there's a
  specific post-freeze-visibility mechanism producing the non-monotonicity."
- **Sanity check on the visibility ceiling** (`self_checked, high confidence`): seed 1001 gives
  bit-identical `diffMean` at radius 4 and 6 (`-0.013825112267544395` to full float precision) —
  not a bug. Its partner-visible tally already hit 38/38 at radius 4, so the frozen agent's
  observation is never masked at either radius and `relativeEntry`'s output is identical between
  them. Useful confirmation the harness behaves exactly as designed once visibility saturates.
- **`QLearningPolicy.act`'s exploration draws from the seeded `Rng`, not `Math.random`**
  (`self_checked, high confidence` — read directly from `src/agent/policy.ts`): a rerun at a fixed
  seed and radius is bit-identical, so there's no within-seed stochasticity to average over. This
  matters for how any future variance-characterization increment would have to be designed (more
  distinct seeds, not repeated runs at one seed).

## Assumptions made (proceeding unless told otherwise)

- `VIEW_RADII = [2, 4, 6]`: chose 6 as a value expected to stay under the whole-episode sweep's
  38/38/38-at-radius-8 ceiling for most seeds. In this post-freeze-only design it didn't fully —
  seed 1001 reached 38/38 (same as radius 4) and seed 1002 reached 37/38, only seed 1003 (33/38)
  left real headroom — because post-freeze-only trajectories land agents in different relative
  positions by `FREEZE_STEP` than the whole-episode sweep did, so the earlier run's per-radius
  visible-count numbers don't transfer to this design. Recorded rather than silently re-picking
  radii mid-run; `postFreezePartnerVisibleCount` makes the actual per-run ceiling visible for any
  future radius choice.
- Placed the `postFreezeEnvMutation` mutation point at "immediately before the `env.step()` call
  producing the `freezeStep` record" (so all 38 post-freeze records reflect it, including the
  first) rather than "immediately after the `freezeStep` record" (37 of 38 reflect it). The
  stricter reading matches "post-freeze-only" most literally and costs one extra conditional in
  `runEpisode`'s loop; no `EpisodeStepRecord` fields needed to change.
