## Learned

- The existing "repeated identical-input training steps drive the loss down" coarse sanity check
  (`test/model/worldModel.test.ts`, pre-existing, `freeBits: 0`) started failing after wiring in the
  reconstruction term: 40 steps / first-5-vs-last-5-average was reliable with the KL loss alone, but
  adding a second, independently-initialized loss term (the decoder) sharing the same optimizer step
  increased per-step variance enough that some seeds' last-5 window landed above their first-5 window
  purely from Gumbel-sampling noise, even though the underlying trend was a clear decrease —
  `self_checked, high confidence` (reproduced directly across 8 seeds at several step counts/window
  widths; 40-step/5-window failed on seed 42 even at 100 steps, but 150-step/20-window decreased
  reliably across every seed tried, including 42 and the two seeds the tests use). Widened both this
  test and the new reconstruction-specific one to 150 steps / first-20-vs-last-20, with a comment
  explaining why, rather than picking a seed that happened to pass at the old width — the old width's
  passing seeds weren't robust to the added variance, they were just lucky at 40 steps specifically.
- Confirmed empirically (not just by writing the type) that `tf.customGrad`/`arraySync()` reads inside
  a `tf.variableGrads`-differentiated `forward()` closure don't disturb the gradient tape: reading the
  reconstruction/KL breakdown values as plain numbers from *inside* `forward()` (needed since only the
  combined scalar is what gets differentiated) and returning only their sum works cleanly — the
  gradient-separation and weight-movement tests below still pass with this pattern —
  `self_checked, high confidence`.
- `@tensorflow/tfjs-node` wasn't installed in this run's fresh container (`node_modules` absent) —
  `npm ci` succeeded cleanly on this Linux x64 sandbox (ADR-0002 decision 6's canonical environment),
  same as every prior run's note that this only affects darwin/arm64 (deprioritized, human-driven
  out-of-band). Not a new finding, just re-confirming the container-to-container variance PR #39's
  review already flagged for its own "verified on CI, not locally" caveat.
