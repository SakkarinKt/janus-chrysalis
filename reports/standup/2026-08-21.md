## Learned

- **The frozen agent's observation-divergence count explains all three seeds' `diffMean`
  outcomes without a new hypothesis** (`self_checked, high confidence` on the counts themselves —
  direct tally over this run's telemetry; `medium confidence` on the interpretation). Seed 1003's
  exact-zero `diffMean` is fully explained: the partner never enters `viewRadius: 2` of the frozen
  agent post-freeze, so `relativeEntry` keeps returning `[0, 0, 0]` in both control and
  intervention regardless of what the partner's action actually is (0/38 observation-divergent
  steps). Seed 1002 gets only 3/38 visible-and-different steps — low instrument power, consistent
  with its near-zero `diffMean`. Seed 1001 — the one seed with a directionally clear `diffMean` —
  has the highest observation-divergence count (24/38), and is the only seed where the frozen
  agent's loss actually had substantial opportunity to see something different.
- **Small numeric correction to the PR #47 review's own count**: this run measured seed 1001's
  frozen-agent observation divergence as 24/38, not the 23/38 the review comment quoted. The
  review's number reads as an informal count made while reading code/telemetry during review, not
  a committed measurement — this run is the first time it's a direct tally over telemetry, so
  24/38 is what's reported and committed. Non-blocking (doesn't change which seed is highest or
  the interpretation), flagged for completeness.
- **This reframes, rather than resolves, the open question from the 2026-08-20 update**: "the
  frozen agent's world-model loss isn't tracking action divergence into a consistent signal" is
  better read as "on 2 of 3 seeds the frozen agent's own observation almost never changed, so
  there was nothing for its loss to track in the first place" — the co-located, `viewRadius`-gated
  observation channel is much narrower than raw joint-action divergence. `n=3` with one seed
  landing enough visible steps to show a directional effect is consistent with "the instrument
  works but needs more visible post-freeze steps per seed," not yet a demonstration either way.
