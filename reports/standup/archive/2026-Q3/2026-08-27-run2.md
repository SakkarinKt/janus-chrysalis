## Learned

- **The apparent radius-4/radius-6 non-replication was substantially a statistical-power
  artifact** (`self_checked, high confidence`): 3 of 9 seeds had low partner-visibility at radius 4
  (1008: 0/38, 1006: 2/38, 1003: 11/38) versus uniformly high visibility (27-38/38) at radius 6 for
  every seed. Filtering to the 6 seeds with real power at both radii flips the picture from "6/9
  same-sign, indistinguishable from chance" to "5/6 same-sign, `p≈0.109`" — still not conventionally
  significant at this small n, but no longer supporting "radius 4 looks nothing like radius 6."
- **Not purely a power story**: seed 1007 has full visibility (38/38) at *both* radii and still
  flips sign (+0.3471 at r4, -0.0453 at r6) — an unexplained counter-example that power alone
  doesn't resolve.
- **Sign-count and magnitude readings diverge**: even restricted to the 6 high-power seeds, the
  continuous paired difference (r4 - r6) has mean +0.0279, `t(5)≈0.37` — null. The signal (if any)
  shows up in sign consistency, not in effect size, at this design and sample size.
- **Meta-lesson for this investigation**: three of the last four review cycles on this axis
  (n=6 interpretation errors, the gate-(b) sign misread, now the power confound) have been
  corrections to *interpretation* of otherwise-correct data, not to the data itself. Worth treating
  future summary paragraphs on this axis with extra scrutiny before writing them, not just after.
