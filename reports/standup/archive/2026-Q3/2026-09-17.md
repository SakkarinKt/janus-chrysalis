## Learned

- **The two acknowledgment comments on PR #74 (`#74#issuecomment-5693302758` and
  `#74#issuecomment-5693309138`), both posted 2026-09-16, restate the same next-run instruction
  twice** rather than one being a correction of the other — read both before acting to catch this;
  treating only the later one as authoritative would have missed nothing here since they agree, but
  that agreement was itself worth confirming rather than assuming.
- **A "Decided from reasoning, checked later by measurement" split (item 5's final form) is a
  pattern worth naming**: when a decision's own inputs don't exist yet (`RewardHead`, here), the
  spec-only increment can still commit to a decision from the estimate, with the empirical check
  explicitly deferred to whichever run can actually produce the number — rather than either
  blocking the decision on that future run, or silently leaving the decision unstated until then.
- `self_checked, high confidence`: the general-form reward bound the PR #74 review offered
  (`≈ -2·numLandmarks - collisionPenalty`) is an approximation that drops the `(gridSize-1)/gridSize`
  factor; the exact form (`numLandmarks * (2*(gridSize-1)/gridSize) + collisionPenalty`) reproduces
  `computeReward()`'s worst case precisely at any `gridSize`, not just approximately, and still
  equals `4.0` under `DEFAULT_CONFIG` — used the exact form in `0014`'s `rewardScale` derivation
  rather than the review's stated approximation, since both were available and the exact form is
  strictly more correct for the "re-checked against a future config change" purpose the review
  named.
