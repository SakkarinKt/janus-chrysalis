## Learned

- **All three new seeds came back negative** (`self_checked, high confidence`): 1007 -0.0453, 1008
  -0.7359, 1009 -0.0195. Pooled n=9: mean(diffMean) = -0.0800, mean(|diffMean|) = 0.2698,
  stddev(diffMean) = 0.4083 (population) / 0.4331 (sample). Sign split is now **8 negative, 1
  positive** — seed 1006 is now the only positive value out of nine.
- **That sign split crosses the conventional significance threshold** (`self_checked, medium
  confidence`, exploratory): under a null of independent random signs, 8-or-more-extreme out of 9
  is two-sided `p ≈ 0.039` (exact binomial). Flagged explicitly as one comparison among several this
  investigation has made (the viewRadius sweep, the landmark-decoupling check, now sign count) —
  read as suggestive given that history, not as a confirmed effect without correcting for the
  earlier looks.
- **The review's "unsaturated seeds have large |diffMean|" lead does not hold** (`self_checked,
  high confidence`): seed 1008 has the second-largest magnitude (-0.7359) and is fully saturated
  (38/38, same visibility state as five small-magnitude seeds), while both outlier-ish seeds (1006
  positive, 1003 large-negative) are unsaturated. Partner-visibility saturation does not separate
  large-magnitude seeds from small ones in this pooled set.
- **The investigation's "leading hypothesis" has now changed three times on the same underlying
  question** (pre-freeze-training confound → landmark-gate confound → sign-consistency read, itself
  corrected once already) — worth keeping in view as a base rate when judging how much weight to
  put on the current n=9 reading.
