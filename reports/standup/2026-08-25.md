## Learned

- **n=6 at radius 6: sign flips persist, and the largest-magnitude swing yet came from a new seed**
  (`self_checked, high confidence` on the numbers). Pooled `diffMean` values: 1001 -0.0138, 1002
  -0.0435, 1003 -0.5103, 1004 -0.0813, 1005 -0.1242, 1006 **+0.8542**. mean(diffMean) = 0.0135,
  mean(|diffMean|) = 0.2712, stddev(diffMean) = 0.4109 — a near-zero pooled mean next to a stddev
  roughly 3x the mean magnitude, with three negative and three positive values spanning -0.51 to
  +0.85.
- **This favors, but does not prove, the sampling-noise reading** (`self_checked, medium
  confidence`): the pattern (mean near zero, large spread, sign-inconsistent, no seed dominating)
  reads more like irreducible per-seed variance than a consistent directional partner-visibility
  effect at this scale. It does not rule out a seed-dependent but non-monotonic *mechanism* (e.g.
  interaction with each seed's specific spawn geometry) that would produce the same statistical
  signature — six seeds can't distinguish "noise" from "a mechanism that varies by seed" on its
  own. Restated as this stand-up's "Decisions needed" item rather than resolved.
- **The override-persistence bug was real but inert**: confirmed by construction (every run to date
  builds a fresh `CooperativeGridWorld` per condition/seed, so no run's numbers were affected) and
  by the new test, which reproduces the exact `setPartnerViewRadius(5)` → `reset()` sequence the
  review described and asserts the override no longer survives.
- **Corrections don't change any headline number**: both proposal-doc corrections narrow *how much
  weight the existing evidence carries*, not the evidence itself — the 12/12 timing-guard result
  and the 1002-only indirect-channel result are the same tallies as 2026-08-24's, just described
  more precisely.
