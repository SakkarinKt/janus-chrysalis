## Learned

- **Confirmed, not just hypothesized: most of 2026-08-12's `diffMean` signal was init noise, not
  a freeze effect** (`self_checked, high confidence`). `diffMean` magnitudes collapsed from
  `[−0.3022, +0.1066, +0.1839]` (unpaired) to `[−0.0936, +0.0036, +0.0000]` (paired) at the exact
  same 3 seeds and freeze point — closely matching @SakkarinKt's PR #45 review estimate of what a
  control-vs-control noise floor under unseeded init would look like ("the same magnitude as the
  reported −0.30/+0.11/+0.18").
- **Fixing init was free beyond just reproducibility — it gave true common-random-numbers pairing
  for the whole pre-freeze phase**, not only initial weights (`self_checked, high confidence,
  directly verified`). `runEpisode`'s `policyRng`/`worldModelRngs` were already `seed`-derived and
  `freezeConfig` never changes which action gets chosen or which RNG value gets drawn pre-freeze —
  only whether `update()`/training runs. So once weight init was the only remaining source of
  divergence, fixing it made every pre-freeze `EpisodeStepRecord` (actions, rewards, observations,
  world-model losses) bit-identical between control and intervention for a given seed. This run's
  `assertPreFreezeParity` diagnostic checks this directly and is `true` for all 3 seeds
  (`artifacts/.../manifest.json`'s `findings[0].preFreezeParityCheck`) — not assumed from the
  RNG-stream argument alone.
- **Still not a clean gate (b) pass, and now more informative about why**
  (`self_checked, medium-high confidence`). What's left after removing init noise is small and
  mixed-sign — not confirming a freeze effect. Seed 1003's `diffMean` is exactly `0.0000`:
  intervention and control produced bit-identical `postFreezeLossSeries`, meaning agent 1's
  post-freeze actions never diverged between "kept training" and "frozen" for that trajectory.
  This firms up (doesn't newly test) 2026-08-12's hypothesis that 37 pre-freeze steps may be too
  few for `QLearningPolicy` to reach post-freeze states where continuing to train vs. not would
  actually pick a different action.
