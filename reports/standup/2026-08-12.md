## Learned

- **The gate read is inconclusive, not a pass.** Gate (a) (control flat): control's
  `postFreezeLossSeries` slope flips sign across the 3 seeds (+0.0086, +0.0354, −0.0071) —
  not a clean "flat" signal, though n=3 can't really distinguish flat-with-noise from a real
  trend either. Gate (b) (intervention rises above control): the per-seed `diffMean` is
  mixed-sign (−0.30, +0.11, +0.18) — no consistent rising signal. `self_checked, medium
  confidence`: this reads as **an underpowered pilot** (only 37 pre-freeze steps of training for
  a from-zero tabular Q-table keyed on a near-continuous observation vector — most steps likely
  hit unseen keys rather than reinforcing a revisited one, so the training partner has barely
  drifted from near-random behavior by the freeze point), not confirmed evidence the measurement
  mechanism itself is broken. Distinguishing those two explanations needs a follow-up run with a
  longer pre-freeze training phase (many episodes before the freeze point, not steps 1-37 of one
  episode) — not decided or sized here; that's the natural next design call.
- **New finding, previously unflagged**: `RSSMCell`/`ObservationDecoder`'s `tf.layers.dense`/
  `gruCell` construction has no `kernelInitializer` seed (`src/model/rssm.ts:95-98`,
  `src/model/decoder.ts:26`) — confirmed directly (two fresh `WorldModel`s, identical config,
  different initial weights) and independently (re-running this script at the same 3 seeds gave
  different numbers both times). `self_checked, high confidence`. This is the same class of gap
  as the 2026-08-03 quality pass's finding #1 (`sampleHard`, fixed 2026-08-04) but in the
  *initializer* path rather than the *sampling* path — `runEpisode`'s `seed` does not make a
  `WorldModel`-involving run fully bit-reproducible today. Not fixed here (touches
  `RSSMCell`/`ObservationDecoder`'s construction API, belongs to whoever next touches that
  surface); recorded in every run's manifest and in proposal `0001`'s update log.
- Mechanically, the instrument itself ran cleanly: no crashes, all losses finite, correctly
  shaped 38-point post-freeze series per run, no tensor-lifecycle regressions (spot-checked
  `WorldModel.step()` in isolation over 150 calls at these dims — `tf.memory().numTensors` flat
  after the first `train=true` call's one-time Adam-slot allocation, confirming the existing
  "confirmed tensor-leak-free" contract holds at Arm-A scale too, not just the smaller dims
  `test/model/worldModel.test.ts` uses).
