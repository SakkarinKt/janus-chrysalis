## Learned

- **Frozen-agent observation-divergence count rises monotonically with `viewRadius`, as
  mechanically expected** (`self_checked, high confidence` — direct tally over this run's
  telemetry): mean divergent steps across the 3 seeds go 9.00/38 (viewRadius 2) → 19.67/38
  (viewRadius 4) → 28.33/38 (viewRadius 8). A wider view radius does raise how often the frozen
  agent's own observation actually differs between control and intervention, confirming the
  mechanical half of the PR #48 review's question.
- **`|diffMean|` does *not* rise monotonically with `viewRadius`** (`self_checked, high
  confidence` on the numbers; `medium confidence` on the interpretation below): mean `|diffMean|`
  across the 3 seeds go 0.0324 (viewRadius 2) → 0.2040 (viewRadius 4) → 0.1068 (viewRadius 8) — up
  then down, not the clean "more visibility ⟹ bigger signal" the review's question might suggest.
  Per-seed detail is noisier still: seed 1003 goes from `diffMean` exactly 0 (viewRadius 2, fully
  explained by zero observation divergence) to +0.4162 (viewRadius 4, the single largest `|diffMean|`
  in the whole sweep) to −0.0993 (viewRadius 8) — a sign flip alongside the magnitude swing.
  **This is very likely a confound flagged in the run's own docstring, not evidence against the
  visibility hypothesis**: `viewRadius` gates the environment during the pre-freeze training
  window (steps 0–37) as well as the post-freeze measurement window, so a larger `viewRadius`
  changes what both agents saw and how their Q-tables ended up shaped by step 38, not only how
  visible the partner is afterward. The sweep as designed cannot separate "does more post-freeze
  visibility raise the signal" from "does a different pre-freeze training regime raise the
  signal" — `n=3` seeds per radius is also too small to distinguish a real non-monotonic effect
  from noise. Answering the review's question cleanly needs the `viewRadius` change to apply
  *after* `freezeStep` only (env `viewRadius` held at the current default of 2 for pre-freeze
  training, switched for the post-freeze window alone) — not implemented here, since it's a design
  change to the harness, not a same-run parameter sweep.
- Action-divergence counts are lower at `viewRadius` 4/8 (17/38 or 21–23/38) than at `viewRadius`
  2 for seeds 1002/1003 specifically, consistent with wider visibility changing pre-freeze-trained
  Q-tables enough to shift which actions the post-freeze exploration lands on — another symptom of
  the same pre-freeze/post-freeze confound above, not something to read as "wider visibility
  reduces action divergence" on its own.

  visibility changes (see Learned), so it doesn't cleanly answer "does raising *visible-step*
  counts raise `|diffMean|`" — only "does raising `viewRadius` overall raise it" (answer: not
  monotonically, `n=3`). Two ways to get a clean read, both bounded increments: (a) a
  post-freeze-only `viewRadius` change (keep pre-freeze training identical across the sweep,
  vary `viewRadius` only from `freezeStep` onward — needs a small `CooperativeGridWorld`/env-config
  change to support a mid-episode config switch), or (b) more seeds at the existing `viewRadius`
  values to see if the non-monotonicity survives a larger `n`. Recommend (a) as the more direct
  answer to the review's actual question, but flagging both for the human's call rather than
  picking unilaterally, since (a) is a small env-interface change and (b) is pure compute — this
  is a design call about which confound to spend the next increment resolving.
